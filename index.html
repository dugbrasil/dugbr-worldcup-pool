DUG Pool Guide
⚽ How to place a bet
  • Go to Matches and select Today
  • Type your predicted score for each team (e.g. Brazil 2 x Morocco 0)
  • Click Place bet, or use Save all bets at the bottom to save everything at once
  • You can update any bet until 15 minutes before kickoff
  • Not sure what to predict? Tap 🔮 for a random score inspired by the seismic velocity model
📊 Points system
Exact score+10
Right winning margin, wrong score+5
Right winner or tie, wrong margin+3

What "right winning margin" means:

Bet 2-1, result 3-2. Both are a 1-goal win for the home team. Same margin, different score. That pays +5.

Bet 2-1, result 4-1. You got the winner right but the margin is different (1 goal vs 3 goals). That pays +3.

Bet 1-1, result 2-2. Both are a tie with zero goal difference. That pays +5.

Tiebreaker: most exact scores wins. Then right margins. Then correct outcomes. Then earliest registration.

📈 About the odds NEW
The win / tie / loss percentages on each match card come from The Odds API, which aggregates prices from major bookmakers including DraftKings, FanDuel, and Bet365. They show the market's implied probability for each outcome, based on where real money is going. Not a guarantee, just the crowd's best guess.
  • A team at 60%+ is a strong favourite. Betting them to win is the safe play.
  • A team at 15-25% is an underdog. But an exact score on an upset still pays +10, same as getting the favourite right. Picking upsets can win you the pool.
  • Odds refresh automatically every 4 hours before each match.
